Arrivals into Australia

The Australian tourism industry is gradually recovering from the COVID-19 pandemic that brought global travel to an unprecedented standstill. International tourism fell sharply in early 2020 and has only slowly recovered since restrictions were lifted in the first half of this year.

This histogram shows the difference in arrival numbers for several popular Countries in 2017 (before COVID-19 lockdowns and restrictions) and 2021 (after COVID-19 restrictions had been lifted). There are minimal arrivals from countries in the year following COVID-19 when compared to the number of arrivals typically seen beforehand. The Australian tourism industry took a massive hit and is still trying to generate numbers simular to those seen in 2017.
